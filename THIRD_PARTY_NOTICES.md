## Adaptation

These are real acoustic drum one-shots, sequenced into the app's original
rhythm patterns. Tempo changes do not change the pitch of the recordings.
Only these seven recordings are bundled; the full library is not included.

Recordings were converted to mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, onset-trimmed,
peak-normalized to −1 dBFS, and shortened with a 60 ms end fade for a small
offline download. Exact sources, checksums, and durations are preserved in
[assets/audio/sources.json](assets/audio/sources.json).
